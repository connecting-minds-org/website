Who We Are

A sophisticated paradigm in counter-extremism.

Connecting Minds Ltd is a newly established non-profit organisation based in the UK. Founded by Sohail Ahmed and Wahidur Rahman, we are driven by a shared commitment to protect vulnerable individuals and communities from the harms of radicalisation.

We believe the most effective way to counter extremist narratives is by equipping individuals with the cognitive tools to deconstruct them. Through a combination of grassroots education, high-level research, and policy advocacy, we bridge the gap between academic theory and practical community safeguarding.

"Our mission is to foster a society where critical thought serves as the primary defense against ideological manipulation."

Our Focus Areas

Comprehensive Strategies for Complex Challenges

We tackle extremism from multiple angles, ensuring a holistic approach to safeguarding and prevention.

Educational Workshops

Delivering targeted workshops to teach critical thinking. We equip individuals with the cognitive tools to question, analyse, and reject extremist propaganda.

Field Research

Conducting rigorous research into the evolving landscape of radicalisation, mapping out how extremist ideologies spread both online and offline.

Policy & Advocacy

Translating our research into action. We advocate for evidence-based policy changes, advising stakeholders on effective counter-extremism frameworks.

Community Outreach

Expanding our counter-extremism work directly into communities. We build grassroots partnerships to create highly resilient local networks.
